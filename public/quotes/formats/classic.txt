tags: love

Love is not blind. It sees everything and decides to stay quiet for a while.

Most people fall in love with the version of you that existed before they knew you.

Love does not fade. It gets outnumbered by small irritations.

You can love someone completely and still be the wrong shape for their life.

The most romantic thing anyone ever did was keep showing up after the romance was gone.

Love is easiest with people who are not yet disappointed in you.

Being adored is a short contract. Being tolerated is the long one.

People say love is patient. Love is mostly exhausted, and polite about it.

Nobody falls out of love in one day. They just notice one day.

The person who loves you most will also know exactly where to hurt you.

Love is a story two people tell until one of them changes the ending.

Most love dies of hunger, not of wounds.

Romance is what you call attention before it becomes a habit.

You do not lose love. You stop paying for it, and it is repossessed.

Love will not save you from loneliness. It only gives loneliness a witness.

We call it a broken heart. It is mostly broken plans.

People do not leave because love ended. Love ends because people already left.

The first year is about who they are. Every year after is about who they are becoming.

Love asks for honesty, then flinches every time it gets some.

Nobody is your other half. Everyone is already whole, and difficult.

tags: relationships

Every relationship is a silent negotiation about who will need whom more.

The quiet person in the relationship has usually already made a decision.

You learn who someone is the first time you are inconvenient to them.

Most couples are not fighting about the dishes. They are fighting about being seen.

People will forgive you for leaving. They will not forgive you for being happier afterwards.

A relationship is two people slowly learning which subjects are dangerous.

The relationship ended the day you started rehearsing conversations in your head.

You cannot argue someone into wanting you.

Compatibility is just two sets of flaws that happen not to collide.

Nobody owes you the reason they stopped trying.

The person who explains the most usually has the most to hide.

Some people only love you in private, where it costs them nothing.

Every argument you avoid becomes a debt with interest.

Attention is the only currency that cannot be faked for long.

People do not change for you. At best they change near you.

If you have to earn their kindness every week, it is not kindness. It is a fee.

Breakups are rarely sudden. They are slow, and then they are official.

You are not hard to love. You are hard to love cheaply.

Most people want honesty until it is about them.

The end of a relationship is mostly paperwork for something that died earlier.

tags: death

Everyone you have ever met is on the same schedule, just with different dates.

Death is not the opposite of life. It is the deadline.

The dead keep their secrets better than the living keep their promises.

At the funeral everyone remembered the same three stories. The rest of his life was gone.

You will die in the middle of something. Everyone does.

The hardest part of death is that the world does not pause to notice.

There is no good age to die. There are only ages at which people are less surprised.

Death makes everyone honest, for about a week.

Your obituary will be shorter than your inbox.

Nobody lies on their deathbed about work. They just stop mentioning it.

Death does not care what you were saving for.

The phone still has their number. Nobody can delete it. Nobody can call it either.

Every unfinished conversation becomes permanent the moment one side dies.

You do not get to choose your last words. You only get to choose how many good ones you said before.

Death is the only problem everyone solves the same way.

Graves are kept tidy by people who did not visit often enough when it mattered.

People fear death because it ends the story. The story ended for most of them years ago.

Mortality is the only fact that makes the rest of the calendar matter.

The dead are not waiting for you. They are not doing anything at all.

Death is ordinary. That is what makes it unbearable.

tags: grief

Grief is the only visitor who keeps a key after you change the locks.

Nobody tells you grief is mostly paperwork and leftovers.

You grieve the person, then the future, then the version of yourself that knew them.

Grief does not get smaller. Your life gets bigger around it, if you let it.

Some losses are not loud. They are just a chair nobody sits in.

People stop asking how you are long before you stop being not fine.

The worst day of grief is not the funeral. It is the first ordinary Tuesday after.

Grief is love that has run out of things to do.

You do not move on. You move with it, more slowly.

Mourning ends officially. Missing does not.

tags: family

Your family has a version of you they will defend against all evidence.

Families are where people practise their first cruelties and call it teasing.

Every family has one person carrying everyone else's silence.

You can leave home and still spend decades answering to it.

Parents give you two things: a story about yourself and a lifetime to correct it.

Some families stay together the way stones stay in a wall: by weight, not by choice.

Your parents' marriage taught you more about love than any book you read.

The holidays are when families remember why they live in different cities.

Children notice everything and understand it all wrong, for years.

You only see your parents clearly when you are older than they were when they hurt you.

Every family has a ledger. Nobody admits to keeping it.

Blood makes you related. Effort makes you family.

Your siblings are the only people who remember the house the way it really was.

Inheritance reveals more about a family than any funeral speech.

Some parents love their children. Some love the idea of having had them.

Home is where your oldest wounds know the way back.

The family story is always told by the person who survived it best.

You will spend your adulthood unlearning things that kept you safe as a child.

Families forgive the loud and punish the honest.

The day your parents need you is the day the roles quietly reverse forever.

tags: friendship

Friendship survives distance only when it was never about convenience.

Some friends are only friends at a certain volume of alcohol.

You learn who your friends are when your news is good, not when it is bad.

Most friendships end without a fight. Someone simply stops starting the conversation.

A friend who only calls when they need something is a client.

The friends of your twenties are rarely the friends of your forties. Nobody announces the change.

Friendship is the only relationship with no paperwork, which is why it ends so quietly.

Real friends tell you the truth and then help you carry it.

Old friends are archives. When they go, part of your past becomes unverifiable.

You outgrow some friends the way you outgrow a coat: suddenly it just does not fit.

Envy wears friendship as a costume more often than you think.

The friend who never disagrees with you is not paying attention.

Friendships do not need constant contact. They need occasional proof.

Nobody tells you that making friends after thirty requires appointments.

A friend is someone whose absence you would actually notice on a Wednesday.

tags: time

Time is not running out. It is running at exactly the same speed it always has. You just noticed.

You have more yesterdays than you think and fewer tomorrows than you plan for.

Later is where most plans go to be forgotten.

Every year goes faster because each one is a smaller part of your life.

The years you wasted did not feel wasted at the time. That is how it works.

Time reveals everything, slowly enough that nobody gets credit for predicting it.

You do not have time. Time has you, on loan.

The clock is the most honest object in your house.

Someday is not a day of the week.

Most people spend their time like they have a refund policy.

The present is the only place anything has ever happened.

Time heals nothing. It just makes the injury old enough to stop discussing.

You will remember very few days of this year. Choose them deliberately.

People talk about killing time as if it were not killing them back.

The best time to fix something was before it became a habit.

Time is generous to the patient and merciless to the waiting.

Every day you postpone becomes a day you cannot get back to.

Calendars are full. Lives are empty. Both can be true.

Time does not wait. It does not chase either. It simply leaves.

You measure your life in years. Life measures it in attention.

tags: work, money

Your job title will not be mentioned at your funeral, unless nothing else can be.

Work will take everything you offer and ask why you did not offer more.

Busy is the most socially acceptable way to avoid your own life.

Money cannot buy love, but poverty can certainly evict it.

The company is not your family. Families do not do annual layoffs.

You are paid for your time, not your loyalty. Do not give away the second for free.

Success is mostly surviving long enough for luck to find you working.

The richest people you know are often the most afraid of losing it.

Debt is a promise your future self never agreed to.

Most careers are a series of accidents told later as a plan.

Money talks. Mostly it says what you were willing to trade.

A raise feels like freedom for about three months.

Nobody on their deathbed wishes they had answered more email.

The meeting could have been an email. Most lives could have been a decision.

Ambition without rest is just a faster way to arrive exhausted.

You will be replaced at work faster than at the dinner table. Act accordingly.

Wealth is mostly the ability to stop asking permission.

Work hard enough at the wrong thing and you will become very good at it.

Money buys comfort. Comfort buys forgetting. Forgetting buys nothing.

Your salary is what they pay you to forget what you wanted to do.

tags: truth, self

The truth rarely arrives all at once. It leaks.

You are not who you say you are. You are what you repeat.

Self-awareness is mostly a list of things you would rather not know.

Most people lie to themselves first, and to others only as a courtesy.

Nobody is the villain in their own story. That is how villains are made.

The things you refuse to look at are steering.

You cannot think your way out of something you behaved your way into.

Honesty is cheap when it costs someone else.

The truth is not cruel. People who deliver it carelessly are.

Your excuses are perfectly reasonable. That is what makes them dangerous.

You already know what you need to do. You are waiting for it to be easier.

Discipline is just remembering what you wanted when it is no longer exciting.

Your habits are a vote on who you will be in ten years.

Pride is fear wearing its best clothes.

Most people do not want advice. They want permission.

The person you are becoming is built entirely out of today.

Confidence is often just ignorance with good posture.

You do not find yourself. You build yourself, badly at first.

The truth about you is in your calendar, not your intentions.

Nobody can hurt you like the voice you agreed with as a child.

tags: society, people

People rarely remember what you said. They remember how small you made them feel.

The crowd always looks confident. Every individual in it is guessing.

Most cruelty is just convenience that never had to explain itself.

People respect boundaries the way water respects sand: until the tide comes.

Applause is the sound of people relieved it was not them.

Everyone is performing. Some are just better rehearsed.

The world does not reward kindness. It occasionally notices it, briefly.

Most people are decent until it costs them something.

Society is a large agreement to not say certain things out loud.

The loudest opinions usually belong to the people who will never be affected.

We judge others by their actions and ourselves by our intentions.

People will believe almost anything, as long as it lets them keep their routine.

Popularity is the art of disappointing no one important.

The internet did not make people cruel. It gave them an audience.

Nobody is watching you as closely as the version of them you imagine.

tags: aging, memory

Getting older is the slow process of becoming the last person who remembers certain things.

Your body files every decision you made at twenty and sends the invoice at fifty.

Old photographs are proof that you were happy and did not know it.

Memory keeps the music and loses the words.

You do not notice you are aging. You notice everyone else is younger.

Nostalgia is grief that learned to smile.

The older you get, the more funerals feel like reunions.

Youth thinks it has time. Age knows it had it.

Some memories are not lost. They are just no longer invited.

Wrinkles are where the weather of your life left its marks.

You become old the day you stop being curious, whatever your age.

The past is a country where everyone you miss still lives.

Your memory is a museum curated by someone who wanted you to feel better.

Age does not bring wisdom. It brings evidence.

The older you get, the more you understand people you once judged.

Nobody tells you that one day your childhood home will belong to strangers.

Every year you lose a little hearing and gain a little understanding of what was never said.

You will forget most of what worried you this year. Your body will not.

Memory is not a library. It is a rumour you keep spreading about yourself.

Old age is not a defeat. It is a long list of things that stopped mattering.
