@hbp
hook: Nobody is coming.
body: Not the mentor, not the lucky break, not the version of you that finally feels ready. The room stays exactly as you leave it.
punchline: The door was never locked.
tags: reality, choices

@hbp
hook: Love is not enough.
body: It does not pay the rent, raise the children, or survive the fourth year of the same argument at the same table. Love is the fuel, not the engine.
punchline: Most marriages end with plenty of fuel left.
tags: love, marriage

@hbp
hook: You will be forgotten.
body: Three generations from now nobody alive will know your voice. Your name may survive on paper, attached to nothing. Every grudge you carried will be carried by no one.
punchline: Spend your grudges accordingly.
tags: death, legacy

@hbp
hook: Your friends are temporary.
body: Most were assigned by geography, schedules and shared complaints. Change one of those and watch the calls become texts, and the texts become birthdays.
punchline: The ones who stay were never about convenience.
tags: friendship

@hbp
hook: Grief does not end.
body: It changes shape. It becomes smaller, quieter, easier to carry in public. It still sits at the table with you on certain dates.
punchline: You do not get over it. You get used to the weight.
tags: grief

@hbp
hook: Nobody is watching.
body: The audience you perform for is mostly imaginary. The people you fear are busy with their own fear. Your mistakes are forgotten faster than you can replay them.
punchline: That is either a tragedy or a permission slip.
tags: self, reality

@hbp
hook: Time is not money.
body: Money comes back. You can lose it, earn it, borrow it, inherit it. Time only leaves, and it leaves at the same speed whether you use it or not.
punchline: Stop trading the scarce thing for the plentiful one.
tags: time, money

@hbp
hook: The truth is boring.
body: It rarely has a villain, a twist or a moral. Most disasters are small neglects that compounded quietly while everyone was looking elsewhere.
punchline: That is why people prefer stories.
tags: truth

@hbp
hook: Your parents did their best.
body: Their best was limited by what they were given, what they feared and how tired they were. That explains a lot. It excuses less than you were taught.
punchline: Both are true at the same time.
tags: family

@hbp
hook: Love ends in small ways.
body: A question not asked. A story not shared. A door closed a little more quietly than before. Nobody notices the last ordinary evening.
punchline: The big fight is only the paperwork.
tags: love, endings

@hbp
hook: Death is not dramatic.
body: It happens in hospitals at four in the morning, between shift changes, while someone is getting coffee. It does not wait for the right words.
punchline: Say them before the coffee.
tags: death

@hbp
hook: Nobody owes you closure.
body: People leave without explaining because explaining is work, and they have already stopped working on you.
punchline: The silence is the explanation.
tags: endings, relationships

@hbp
hook: Your potential is expiring.
body: Every year you call yourself promising, the word means a little less. Eventually it becomes polite: what people say about someone who never did.
punchline: Promise is a perishable good.
tags: ambition, time

@hbp
hook: You are not special.
body: Your pain is common, your story is familiar and your fears are shared by millions who will never meet you.
punchline: That is not an insult. It is the only reason anyone can help.
tags: self, reality

@hbp
hook: Most people stay.
body: Not because they are happy, but because leaving requires a plan, a fight and a version of themselves they have not met yet.
punchline: Staying is the default. Leaving is a decision.
tags: relationships, choices

@hbp
hook: The world is not fair.
body: It was never designed to be. Fairness is a human invention, enforced unevenly, mostly by the people who benefit from the current version.
punchline: Expect nothing from the universe and specific things from people.
tags: reality, society

@hbp
hook: Love is a risk.

punchline: You are only choosing which ending.
tags: love, grief

@hbp
hook: Your work will not love you back.

punchline: Know who will be at your funeral.
tags: work, death

@hbp

punchline: Choose your losses on purpose.
tags: life, choices

punchline: Help, then let go.
tags: relationships

@hbp

punchline: Write some of it down.
tags: aging, memory

@hbp
hook: The past was not better.

punchline: Nobody is looking after you now.
tags: past, aging

@hbp
hook: Money changes people.

punchline: Watch what people do with a little.
tags: money, people

@carousel
hook: Comfort is a loan.
body: It feels free when you take it. The interest is invisible for years. Then one morning you notice the calls you stopped making and the rooms you no longer enter. Nobody sent a bill. You paid in time.
punchline: Every loan is repaid in the currency you value most.
tags: comfort, time

@carousel
hook: Your funeral will be short.
body: Two hours, maybe three. A few people will speak, and most of what they say will be true and incomplete. Someone will mention your laugh. Someone will check the time. Afterwards there will be food and conversations about traffic. Within a week, the people who loved you will be back at work. That is not cruelty. It is the arrangement.
punchline: Live for the few who will still talk about you a year later.
tags: death, legacy

@carousel
hook: Love is a maintenance job.
body: It is not a feeling you find. It is a building you keep repairing. The roof leaks in winter. The pipes freeze in the fourth year. Someone has to call the plumber, and someone has to pay. Most couples do not split over betrayal. They split over who stopped fixing things.
punchline: Romance is the brochure. Maintenance is the house.
tags: love, marriage

@carousel
hook: You are running out of summers.
body: If you are forty, you may have forty left. If you are sixty, perhaps twenty. Each one feels endless while it is happening. Then it is a photograph, then a vague warmth, then nothing in particular.
punchline: Count them. Counting does not make them shorter. It makes them visible.
tags: time, aging

@carousel
hook: People will disappoint you.
body: Not because they are bad, but because you built them out of hopes they never agreed to carry. Friends are not therapists. Parents are not finished. Partners are not rescuers. Everyone is busy surviving their own week.
punchline: Lower the statue. Keep the person.
tags: people, relationships

@carousel
hook: Nobody remembers the effort.
body: They remember the result, and even that only briefly. The nights you stayed late are visible to you alone. The work that failed is filed under nothing. History is a list of outcomes written by people who were not there.
punchline: Do it for your own record. Nobody else is keeping one.
tags: work, legacy

@carousel
hook: Death keeps no schedule.
body: It does not wait for the reconciliation, the apology or the trip you were saving for. It arrives on a Tuesday, unannounced, in the middle of a sentence. Every unsaid thing becomes permanent at once.
punchline: The only safe time to say it is too early.
tags: death, regret

@carousel
hook: Your parents are aging faster than you notice.
body: You see them twice a year and the change looks sudden. It is not. It was gradual and daily, while you were busy. One visit you notice the handrail. Another, the repeated story. Another, the call that comes at night.
punchline: The visits are numbered. Nobody tells you the number.
tags: family, aging, time

@carousel
hook: Loneliness is crowded.
body: You can be lonely in a marriage, in an office, in a group chat of forty people. Loneliness is not a lack of company. It is a lack of being known by anyone in the room.
punchline: More people will not fix it. Fewer, deeper ones might.
tags: loneliness

@carousel
hook: The truth costs something.
body: Every honest sentence has a price: a friendship, a promotion, a comfortable silence at dinner. Most people pay a small fee in lies every day to avoid the larger bill. The bill arrives anyway, with interest.
punchline: Pay early. It is cheaper.
tags: truth
